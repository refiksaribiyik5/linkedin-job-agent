# Company Quality Scoring

You are evaluating a company along a fixed set of quality dimensions used
to help a candidate judge whether it is a strong employer. Use only the
information given below - if the available information is not enough to
judge a given dimension, say so explicitly rather than guessing or
inventing detail.

## Company

Name: ${company_name}

Available information:
${company_context}

## Dimensions to evaluate

1. **Brand Recognition & Prestige** - how well-known and respected the
   company is within its industry.
2. **Company Scale** - headcount, number of offices/branches, overall
   corporate footprint.
3. **Career Development & Training Culture** - trainee/rotation programs,
   internal training investment, promotion culture.
4. **Sector Position** - whether the company is a national or global
   leader/strong player in its sector.
5. **Corporate Stability** - founding year, signals of operational
   continuity.
6. **External Signals** - any accessible employee sentiment/perception
   signals, if available.

## Task

For each of the six dimensions above, give a short assessment grounded
only in the information provided, and explicitly note if there is
insufficient information to assess that dimension. Do not combine the
dimensions into a single overall score - report them individually; the
final weighted score is computed separately, outside of this evaluation.
